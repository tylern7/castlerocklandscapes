About CastleRock

Family-owned, and proud of every yard

CastleRock Landscapes was founded in the winter of 2018 by George Georgakopoulos and Spencer Merrick. We are a family-owned and operated business, and our team brings over 35 years of combined experience in landscape construction.

We stay ahead of industry trends, use the latest tools and keep exploring new product lines. A job isn’t finished until both you and the CastleRock team are thrilled with the result.

  • A 1 to 3 year warranty on all completed projects (product warranties, like interlock, vary by manufacturer)
  • Built with quality materials such as Permacon interlock
